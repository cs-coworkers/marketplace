# Weekly review

Accelerators impose a weekly number and a weekly meeting; founders without one drift. This review
is that meeting (YC Startup School: one primary metric, weekly target, typically 5–10% a week at the
early stage, https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-2-recap-michael-seibel-adora-cheung-and-ilya-volodarsky).

## First time: set up the metric
Ask, one at a time:
1. What one number best shows customers getting value? (revenue, paying customers, weekly active
   users doing the core action). Push back on vanity numbers (signups, page views, cumulative totals).
2. Its exact definition: what counts, what doesn't, the source, and who reads it each week.
3. The weekly growth target. Recommend 5–7% a week pre-revenue to early revenue; say why.
4. Up to 3 secondary metrics (cash burn is always one, from `cash.md`).
Propose the definitions block for `metrics.md`; write after a yes.

## Every week (keep it to 10 minutes)
1. **Read** `metrics.md`, `hypotheses.md`, `decisions.md` (last week's commitments), `now.md`.
2. **The number.** Ask for this week's value (or read the source the founder named). Show: this week,
   target, hit or miss, and the last 10 weeks as a short table. Compute the compounded weekly
   growth over 4 weeks.
3. **Last week's commitments.** For each: done, not done, and why, in one line.
4. **Experiments.** For each live test in `hypotheses.md`: what the evidence says, and a call:
   **kill** (evidence against or no signal after its timebox), **continue** (signal, not yet enough),
   or **double down** (clear signal; put more behind it). Recommend one, with confidence.
5. **Biggest obstacle.** Ask: what is the one thing most in the way of the number? Challenge the
   answer if it isn't about customers or the product.
6. **Next week:** 1–3 commitments, each tied to the number or an experiment. Fewer is better.
7. **Propose** the record rows: metric value, experiment status changes, commitments and calls in
   `decisions.md`. Write after a yes.

## Patterns to name
- Three misses in a row: say so, and ask whether the target, the metric or the strategy is wrong.
  Offer **pivot-review** if it is the strategy.
- Commitments that keep rolling over: name the pattern once, kindly, and cut the list.
- Activity without customer contact for 2+ weeks: say so.
- Growth driven by one customer or one channel: flag the concentration.

## Rules
- Never change a metric definition silently; a new definition is a dated decision.
- Don't fill a missed number; record it as missing.
