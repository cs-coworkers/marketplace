# Raise prep

A raise is a project with a start date set by runway, not by mood. This skill plans it and then walks
the checklist in `raise-checklist.md` (in this skill's folder) for whatever part the founder is in.

## Steps
1. **Read** `cash.md`, `metrics.md`, `hypotheses.md`, `investors.md`, `decisions.md`, `goals.md`.
   If `cash.md` is older than 35 days, run **runway** first.
2. **Raise now?** Compare the raise-by date to today, and the evidence to the bar for the stage
   (run **plan-read** if there's no recent read). Answer one of: raise now; raise by <date> after
   <milestone>; don't raise (default alive, or not yet fundable: name the gap). Confidence stated.
3. **Round size** from the bottom up: the milestones the next round needs (from the stage bar), the
   months to reach them plus 6–9 months of buffer, times the planned burn from **runway**. Show
   the math. Then check the dilution it implies (checklist §2); 15–25% per round is typical.
4. **Instrument:** SAFE (most pre-seed), priced round (most seed above a few $M and every A),
   bridge (checklist §4), or non-dilutive first (checklist §5). Explain the trade-off; the lawyer
   drafts.
5. **Target list method:** the investor profile that fits (stage, check size, sector, lead or
   follow, geography), where to find them, and warm-intro paths from the founder's network. Build
   the first 30–50 rows in `investors.md` with the founder; rank by fit × access. Mark leads vs followers.
6. **Materials:** deck (10–12 slides: problem, customer, solution, traction, market, model, team,
   competition, the raise and use of funds), the model and runway, a one-paragraph email blurb,
   and the data room index (checklist §8). Alex drafts substance; design and copy polish are the founder's or Sam's.
7. **Plan the raise:** a dated plan (prep weeks, outreach window, meeting window of 4–6 weeks, target
   close), weekly pipeline review (checklist §6), and the investor-update cadence to prospects.
8. **Walk the checklist** section by section as the raise moves; tick items in the founder's copy.
   Offer to save a copy to `output/YYYY-MM-DD-raise-checklist.md` with the sections that apply.
9. **Propose** `investors.md` rows, the plan in `output/`, and a `decisions.md` line for raise now /
   size / instrument. Write after a yes.

## Rules
- Mechanics and norms, not legal or investment advice. Documents go to the founder's lawyer (or
  Devorah); tax to their CPA. Never tell the founder which investor's money to take as financial
  advice; give the decision framing and your read, with confidence.
- Alex never contacts investors; it drafts, the founder sends.
- Market figures (round sizes, caps, dilution) only with source and date.
