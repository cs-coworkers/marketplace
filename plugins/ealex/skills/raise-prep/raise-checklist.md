# Raise checklist

The work around a raise that founders need done right but not every month. Alex walks the founder
through the section that applies, using the records; each line is a check, not a skill. Links are
starting points; Alex says when a figure is dated.

Legend: **[Alex]** Alex can do this with the founder from the records · **[Lawyer]** documents and
filings go to the founder's lawyer (or Devorah) · **[CPA]** tax and accounting go to the founder's accountant.

## 1. Metrics ready for diligence (before any raise)
- [ ] One written definition per metric in `metrics.md`, used the same way in the deck, model and updates. [Alex]
- [ ] Check against the common mistakes: bookings vs revenue, one-time fees in ARR, gross vs net burn,
      gross vs net churn, CMGR instead of averaged growth, blended vs paid CAC, "active" defined,
      no cumulative charts. a16z, 16 Startup Metrics: https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/ [Alex]
- [ ] Cohort retention table (logo and revenue), net and gross revenue retention, burn multiple, CAC
      payback, gross margin (AI companies: include inference and compute cost). [Alex]
- [ ] Compare to the bar for the round you're raising. CRV, Series A metrics (2026):
      https://www.crv.com/content/series-a-metrics-vcs-expect · Carta State of Private Markets:
      https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q1-2026-full-report/ [Alex]

## 2. Cap table and SAFE stack (each new instrument, and before a priced round)
- [ ] Current cap table in one place, with every SAFE and note: amount, cap, discount, pre or post-money. [Alex]
- [ ] Model conversion of all SAFEs at the next priced round; show founder ownership after seed and
      after A. Post-money SAFEs stack: each one dilutes founders, not earlier SAFE holders.
      YC SAFE documents and user guide: https://www.ycombinator.com/documents [Alex]
- [ ] Compare to medians (founders hold a median ~56% after seed, ~36% after A; dilution ~16% per
      priced round; Carta, 2025–26): https://carta.com/data/founder-ownership-2026-full-report/ [Alex]
- [ ] Size the option pool from a 12–18 month hiring plan, not the investor's round number; the pool
      in the pre-money lowers your effective valuation. LTSE, the option pool shuffle:
      https://ltse.com/insights/funding-your-startup-the-impact-of-the-option-pool-shuffle [Alex]
- [ ] Instruments drafted and reviewed by counsel. [Lawyer]

## 3. Term sheets and SAFE offers (when offers arrive)
- [ ] Normalize each offer: headline vs effective pre-money (after pool), amount, ownership after the round. [Alex]
- [ ] Exit table: founder proceeds at 3–4 exit values for each offer, with the liquidation preference applied. [Alex]
- [ ] Red-flag terms: preference above 1x or participating, full-ratchet anti-dilution, cumulative
      dividends, loss of founder board majority, broad protective provisions, pitching partner ≠ board partner.
      CRV: https://www.crv.com/content/investor-due-diligence [Alex flags; Lawyer reviews]
- [ ] Decision memo: price vs partner vs terms, with what you'd trade. Record in `decisions.md`. [Alex]

## 4. Bridge or extension (when runway < 12 months and no round is lined up)
- [ ] Run **runway** scenarios: cut to default alive, bridge from insiders, priced round now. [Alex]
- [ ] Bridges are common now (~29% of seed and A rounds in 2025, mostly SAFEs or notes):
      https://carta.com/data/linkedin-bridge-rounds-shift-after-2021/ [Alex]
- [ ] Bridge pitch to current investors: what milestone the money buys, by when, and why it lifts the
      next round. [Alex drafts; founder sends]
- [ ] Instrument terms (cap, discount, MFN). [Lawyer]

## 5. Non-dilutive money (check once a year, and before each raise)
- [ ] SBIR/STTR: federal R&D awards; reauthorized in 2026 through 2031 with new per-company limits
      and foreign-ties screening (confirm current status). https://www.sbir.gov ·
      https://www.iedconline.org/news/2026/04/01/federal-policy-updates/congress-reauthorizes-the-small-business-innovation-research-sbir-and-small-business-technology-transfer-sttr-programs/ [Alex finds topics and drafts; founder submits]
- [ ] R&D tax credit: US startups can offset up to $500K a year of payroll tax; 2025 law restored
      immediate R&D expensing. Keep a running log of R&D work and costs from day one.
      https://frazierdeeter.com/insights/article/start-ups-returning-to-rd-tax-credit-opportunities-under-obbba/ [Alex keeps the log; CPA files]
- [ ] Revenue-based financing once revenue is steady:
      https://www.lightercapital.com/blog/revenue-based-financing [Alex compares cost vs equity]
- [ ] Grants and programs for your sector and region (state, EU, foundation). [Alex searches]

## 6. Investor pipeline (daily or weekly during a raise)
- [ ] Every target in `investors.md` with stage: researching, intro asked, first meeting, partner
      meeting, diligence, term sheet, passed (with the reason). [Alex]
- [ ] Follow-ups due flagged each week; stale leads (no reply in 10 days) get one follow-up draft. [Alex drafts; founder sends]
- [ ] Run meetings in a tight window (4–6 weeks) so offers land together.
- [ ] If you already use an investor CRM (Visible, Foundersuite, OpenVC), keep it there and let Alex
      read it; Visible has a Claude connector: https://visible.vc/mcp-server/ [Alex]

## 7. Check the investor before you sign (after a term sheet)
- [ ] Two references from the firm's list, plus 2–3 you find yourself, including a founder whose
      company failed. Ask: "Would you take their money again?" and "What should I have asked?" [Alex drafts questions]
- [ ] Partner history (prior firms, board behavior in hard times), fund age and reserves for your
      follow-on. SEC adviser search (Form ADV): https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/ ·
      CRV: https://www.crv.com/content/investor-due-diligence [Alex researches]
- [ ] Is the partner a full GP likely to stay? Any portfolio company that competes with you? [Alex]

## 8. Data room (before first partner meetings)
- [ ] Seed: deck, model and runway, cap table and SAFE summary, metrics with definitions, customer
      evidence (interview synthesis, pilots, LOIs), team and hiring plan. [Alex builds the index]
- [ ] Series A adds: cohort data, unit economics, pipeline, key contracts, corporate documents, IP
      assignments, employee agreements. [Alex for numbers; Lawyer for corporate and IP]
- [ ] Gap check: list what's missing from the founder's Drive against the index. [Alex]
- [ ] Pre-write answers to the usual follow-ups: market-size method, churn, CAC, why now, competition. [Alex]
- [ ] Vendor checklists for reference (vendor content, not neutral):
      https://papermark.com/blog/startup-data-room-checklist · https://www.peony.ink/blog/seed-round-data-room

## 9. Pitch rehearsal (each raise or program)
- [ ] Run **plan-read** first; fix evidence gaps before story gaps. [Alex]
- [ ] Mock partner meeting tuned to the named fund's thesis and portfolio, with objections from your
      own numbers; then the 2-minute version. [Alex]
- [ ] Decks get under 3 minutes of reading; order slides for that. DocSend:
      https://www.docsend.com/index/startup-fundraising/ [Alex]

## 10. Board and advisors (quarterly once there is a board; earlier for advisors)
- [ ] Board pack from the records: cash and runway, metrics vs plan, top 3 issues, decisions needed. [Alex]
- [ ] Send 3–5 days ahead; use the meeting for the decisions, not the update. [Alex drafts]
- [ ] Before a board: map advisor gaps (sales, hiring, domain) and specific asks for each advisor. [Alex]
- [ ] Board consents and minutes. [Lawyer]
