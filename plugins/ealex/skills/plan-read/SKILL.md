# Plan read

Read the plan the way the investor who'd write the next check will read it: fast, skeptical, looking
for a reason to say no. Most decks get under 3 minutes of attention (DocSend,
https://www.docsend.com/index/startup-fundraising/). The read's job is to find the reasons before
the investor does.

## Steps
1. **Get the document** (file in the Start folder, Drive link, or pasted). Read all of it.
2. **Read the records:** `cash.md`, `metrics.md`, `hypotheses.md`. Every number the deck states is
   cross-checked against them; mismatches go first in the read.
3. **Set the stage** from the evidence, not the founder's label: idea, pre-seed (team + insight,
   little traction), seed (early revenue or strong usage, repeatable signal), Series A (repeatable
   growth, unit economics). Say the stage you read and why; investors judge each stage on different things.
4. **Score each dimension 1–5** for that stage, one line of reasoning each:
   1. **Problem:** real, urgent, costly; evidence it's felt (not asserted).
   2. **Customer:** a specific first customer (beachhead), not "everyone".
   3. **Solution and insight:** why this works and what the founders know that others don't.
   4. **Traction:** the right metric for the stage, its trend, and its quality.
   5. **Market:** bottom-up size from the beachhead out; top-down only as a check.
   6. **Business model and unit economics:** price, margin, CAC and payback where there's data.
   7. **Team:** why these people win this; gaps named.
   8. **The raise:** amount, milestones it buys, runway it gives (from `cash.md`), use of funds.
5. **Overall read:** one of **not yet**, **close**, **fundable at this stage**, with confidence.
   Weight the dimensions that matter for the stage (team and insight at pre-seed; traction and unit
   economics at seed and A).
6. **Top three gaps**, ranked by how likely each is to produce a "no", each with the specific fix and
   whether it is a story fix (deck) or an evidence fix (go get it; route to **idea-test**).
7. **Likely investor questions:** the 5 hardest, with what the founder's best answer would need.
8. **Save** to `output/YYYY-MM-DD-plan-read-<doc>.md` after a yes. Propose `hypotheses.md` rows for
   any premise the plan assumes but the records don't support.

## Rules
- Candid, specific, kind. Name the strongest part too.
- Benchmarks only with source and date; otherwise say "I don't have current data".
- Not investment advice and not a valuation opinion; it is a read of readiness.
- Deck design and copy polish are not the job; substance is. Marketing copy goes to Sam.
